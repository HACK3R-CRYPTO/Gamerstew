# Word Ladder

Turn one four-letter word into another, one letter at a time. Every step has to be a real word.

## Three ways to play

| Round | Length | Score | Hints |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solo (ranked for a signed-in player) | Three minutes on the clock. Ladders keep coming and get harder | **No ceiling.** Every ladder solved inside the clock | None. Stuck means skip |
| Daily | The same five ladders for everyone that day. One try | Out of 100 | Yes, 3 points each |
| Friend room | Five ladders, the same for everyone in the room | Out of 100 | Yes, 3 points each |

Until 26 November 2026 a solo round was five ladders too, so every good player ended on or near 100 and the board could not tell them apart. The owner's rule is that a ranked score is never capped.

## The timed round

- The clock runs only while a ladder is open, not during the short pause after a solve.
- Ladders 1 to 5 are the old round (two, three, three, four, four steps at best). Then two more of four, four of five, and six from the twelfth on.
- A ladder is worth up to 20: 10 for solving it, 5 for the shortest route (one less for each extra step), 5 for speed (one less every 20 seconds).
- Skipping costs nothing but the time already spent.
- When the clock runs out, the ladder on screen is handed in as it stands and scores nothing.
- The server deals sixty ladders from the run's ticket. Sixty in three minutes is one every three seconds, which nobody can type, so the deal never runs out for a person.

There are no hints in the timed round because a hint there would turn taps into points: a fully hinted ladder used to be worth 14, and with no limit on ladders that is a score anyone could farm.

## How the server checks a round

The server never trusts a score. The page sends the words typed for each ladder and the time each took. The server deals the same ladders again from the ticket and replays them: every word must be real, each step changes one letter, no word is used twice, the route reaches the target, the times fit inside the time the server saw pass, and in a timed round they fit inside three minutes. The total is the server's.

A page asks for the timed round when it opens a run (`climb`) and says so again when it hands the run in (`ladderClimb`). A page that asks for neither, such as an older one still open on a phone, gets the five ladders it expects.

What this cannot prove is that a person found the route. A run typed faster than a person can is scored and flagged in the server log, and prizes are for verified players.

## Leaving

The back arrow in a ranked round asks once, then hands in what was solved. A round left with nothing solved is not handed in.
