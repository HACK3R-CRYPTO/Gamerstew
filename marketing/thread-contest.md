# Thread contest · 2,000,000 G$

Separate from the rope and from the in-app recruiter contest. This one rewards
people who write about GameArena publicly, not people who play.

## Prize split

Top 6, because paying 3 out of a large field means most entrants play for
nothing and stop after one post.

| Place | Prize |
|---|---|
| 1 | 600,000 G$ |
| 2 | 450,000 G$ |
| 3 | 350,000 G$ |
| 4 | 250,000 G$ |
| 5 | 200,000 G$ |
| 6 | 150,000 G$ |
| **Total** | **2,000,000 G$** |

## Quote the prize in G$ only, never in dollars

The internal figure is about 200 USD. Do NOT put a dollar number in any public
post. Two reasons.

The rate moves. At the rate we last used, 430,000 G$ was 50 USD, which puts
2,000,000 G$ nearer 232 USD today, and it will be a different number next week.
Publishing a USD figure converts a fixed G$ prize into a moving promise, and if
G$ moves the wrong way we get accused of cutting the prize when we did not.

It also reads badly to the GoodDollar community, who are the audience most
likely to amplify this. Quoting their currency in dollars signals you do not
think it is real money.

Say "2,000,000 G$". Let people value it themselves.

## Rules

1. Post publicly about GameArena on X. A thread, not a single tweet.
2. Tag @Gamearenahq so we can find it. Untagged posts cannot be judged.
3. Say something true. Screenshots of your own play, your own team, your own
   score, what you actually think.
4. Posting window runs to the end of the event.
5. One entry per person. Your best thread counts, not your total.

## Judging, stated up front

Not engagement count. Engagement is buyable and a bought-engagement winner
poisons the next contest.

Judged on: does it explain what GameArena actually is, is it honest, would
someone who has never heard of us understand it, and did it bring anyone in.
Referral link in the thread is a tiebreaker, not a requirement.

Judges: the GameArena team. Decisions published with the winners so the
reasoning is visible.

## What not to promise

Do not say "guaranteed" anywhere in this contest. The 2,500 G$ bounty is
guaranteed because it is first come and capped. This is a judged contest with
6 places, and calling any part of it guaranteed sets up the same confusion the
WhatsApp group already had about the bounty.
