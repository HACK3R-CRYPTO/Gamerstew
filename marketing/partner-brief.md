## Questions your community will ask

**"Do I need money to start?"**
No. Free to play, free to verify.

**"Do I need a wallet?"**
No. Sign in with Google and one is made for you.

**"What is the face scan for?"**
It proves every player is a real separate person. It is what stops one person
farming the prizes with twenty accounts.

**"Is the 2,500 G$ certain?"**
Yes, for the first 160 who verify and play 3 games. It does not depend on
winning.

**"What if my team loses?"**
They keep the 2,500, and the losing team still shares part of the pot.

**"Can I pick my team?"**
No. It is assigned when you verify. Arrive through someone's link and you join
their team.

**"What is G$?"**
GoodDollar, a digital currency on Celo. Be straight with people: GoodDollar is
restoring exchange liquidity after an exploit, so converting it is limited
right now. The prizes are real and land in their wallet either way.

## Please do not say

- **Do not promise a specific team payout.** The team share depends on how many
  people join. "The two teams share 600,000" is safe. "You will get 10,000" is
  not.
- **Do not say people cannot use more than one wallet.** The accurate claim is
  one face, one scoring player.
- **Do not invent games.** The four are listed above. MARKOV plays rock paper
  scissors.
- **Do not tell people to verify a child, or anyone else's face.** One person,
  one account, adults only. Accounts doing that are removed from the prizes.
