# GameArena · Season highlights for Rael (to read before presentations)

Short, evidence-backed, all on-chain. Numbers pulled live 2026-09-29 11:34 UTC
(subgraph + payout ledger); full snapshot in demo-day-6-metrics.md.

- **Demo Day 1 → now, on the same chart.** Players 227 → 879 (3.9×). Games on-chain
  12,483 → 61,966 (5×). G$ to the GoodDollar UBI pool 385 → 45,769 (119×). Nothing
  self-reported — every figure is on the subgraph and at gamearenahq.xyz/impact.

- **Verified humans are the moat.** 163 verified humans (19%) — every game and prize
  gated by GoodDollar proof-of-personhood. Zero bots ever paid. This is the thing no
  competitor has.

- **Every GoodBuilders target beaten.** 879 players (target 200, 4.4×), 163 verified
  humans (target 75, 2.2×), 45,769 G$ to UBI (target 2,500, 18×), 2.1M G$ to players
  (target 400K, 5×). All auditable on the PerkShop contract and the Dune dashboard.

- **Over 2,100,000 G$ paid to verified players** across four competitions this season
  (2,106,455 G$ + $50 USDC, 43 player slots) — all on-chain, every payout public with
  its transaction at gamearenahq.xyz/impact.

- **45,769 G$ routed to the GoodDollar UBI pool** (perk shop 24,384 + habitat unlocks
  21,385, both indexed on-chain) against a 2,500 G$ target — 18×. Total in-app G$ spend
  is 228,522 G$ across 3,422 perk purchases and 11 habitat unlocks.

- **The events engine is the game-changer.** We launched Tug of War — a 7-day team event
  with 2,000,000 G$ in prizes — where you only score by bringing verified humans. Growth
  and verification became the same motion; the event markets proof-of-personhood, not the
  games, and that rebuilt the verified base.

- **Made the numbers provable.** Removed 896 sybil matches from wallets with no GamePass,
  gated ranking on a minted pass, moved scoring server-side. The traction above is
  provably real people, not inflation.

- **Partnerships that extend reach.** GoodAgents (players deploy their own AI agents into
  the arena, built jointly with a fellow GoodBuilders project), and a BlockHub creator
  contest (external creators bring players).

- **What's next, past the season:** the social rails are shipped — friend duels (stake G$,
  winner takes 80%, 20% to UBI) and voice MARKOV. Next is scale: Month 3 targets of 200
  wallets, 75 verified humans, 30 settled duels, 5 GoodCollectives — then GameArena's games
  embedded inside a partner chat app's DMs, and a MiniPay listing.
