## The one-line brief

GameArena is running a 7-day team tug of war on Celo where every player is a
GoodDollar face-verified human, and we are paying 2,000,000 G$ to the six best

## What GameArena is, for someone who has never heard of it

A web game arena you play on any phone, no download. Three skill games, plus an
AI agent called MARKOV you can play against. Every game is a transaction on
Celo, so the leaderboard is not something we type in, it is on-chain and anyone
can verify it.

The thing that makes it different from every other play-to-earn: you cannot
enter without passing GoodDollar face verification. One human, one account. No
bot has ever been paid.

Live numbers, all verifiable at dune.com/ogazboiz/gamearena:
788 players, 59,360 games played on-chain, 21,385 G$ routed into the UBI pool.

## The event to write about

Two teams, Red and Blue, pulling one rope for 7 days. The system assigns your
side and keeps the teams level, so nobody picks and nobody can stack a team.

What moves the rope: how well you play, not how much. Only your best score each
day counts, so quitting and restarting earns nothing. Bringing a verified
player is worth 20 points to your side, wherever the system puts them.

The first 160 people to verify and play 3 games get 2,500 G$ each, guaranteed,
whether their side wins or loses.

## Angles that are actually true

Pick one, do not try to cover all of them.

**The bot problem.** Every airdrop and play-to-earn gets farmed by wallets.
GameArena gates on face verification, so the prize pool goes to people. This is
the strongest angle and the least written about.

**Skill, not grinding.** Most crypto games pay for volume, which rewards whoever
can run a script longest. Here only your best run each day counts. A good player
beats a busy one.

**It runs on a cheap Android.** No download, no wallet setup ceremony, works on
the phones people in the Global South actually own. Celo fees are sub-cent.

**The AI opponent.** MARKOV plays Rock Paper Scissors and Coin Flip against
players and has fired thousands of its own on-chain transactions.

## Rules for creators

Say it in your own words. A thread that reads like a press release converts
nobody and will not win the contest.

Screenshots beat claims. Your team, your score, the rope.

Tag @Gamearenahq so we can find and reshare it.

Do not promise returns, do not call it an investment, do not quote a dollar
value for G$. Say the prize in G$.

## What NOT to say

Do not say players are guaranteed to win money. The only guaranteed thing is the
2,500 G$ bounty for the first 160, and it is capped and first come.

Do not describe G$ as a token to trade. It is UBI. The audience most likely to
care about this event is the GoodDollar community and they will not take kindly
to it being framed as a trade.

Do not invent features. Three skill games and MARKOV. MARKOV plays Rock Paper
Scissors and Coin Flip, not dice.
