# WhatsApp: the simple version

Real feedback from the group on the first send: "I no understand all."

That version explained the scoring, both prize pools, and the difference
between a race and a contest. Three ideas too many. Nobody reads a rulebook in
a group chat.

**Send ONE idea: free money for being early.** The teams, the points and the
recruiter prize are for people who already said yes. They are not the hook.

WhatsApp bold is `*one asterisk*`. Copy the blocks exactly.

```
🪢 *TUG OF WAR* starts Wednesday

*The first 160 people get 2,500 G$ each. Free.*

To get yours:
1. Open gamearenahq.xyz
2. Verify your face (30 seconds, one time only)
3. Play 3 games

That is all. No competition. No waiting. Just be one of the first 160.

*And the 2,500 is only the start.* After that you join Red or Blue, and the two teams share another *600,000 G$* when the 7 days finish. Both teams get paid, the winners get more.

Do it *tonight*. When the 160 is finished, it is finished.

👉 gamearenahq.xyz
```

Why this works: one promise, three steps, one deadline, and a single line saying
there is more after it.

That line matters. Without it people read "2,500 G$" and assume that is the
whole event, so anyone who misses the 160 thinks there is no reason to show up.
It says "and there is more" without explaining points, teams or the recruiter
prize, which is what confused the group the first time.

Do NOT add a per-person figure for the team share. It depends on how many
people qualify, and a number you cannot guarantee is a number you will be held
to. "Both teams share 600,000" is true whatever the turnout.

## If someone asks a question, answer with one line

**"How do I join a team?"**
```
You do not pick. When you verify, the system gives you Red or Blue and keeps both sides even. Even a friend's link does not change your team. What it does change is points: anyone you bring adds 20 points to YOUR side, whichever side they end up on.
```

**"Is the 2,500 G$ sure?"**
```
Yes. First 160 people who verify and play 3 games. It does not depend on winning anything.
```

**"What if my team wins?"**
```
Your team shares 400,000 G$. How much you get depends on how much you pulled for the team, so playing and bringing people both count.
```

**"What if my team loses?"**
```
You still keep your 2,500 G$, and your team still shares 200,000 G$. Losing pays less, it does not pay nothing.
```

**"So is it only 2,500 G$?"**
```
No. 2,500 is just for being one of the first 160. On top of that the two teams share 600,000 G$, and the person who brings the most people wins 500,000 G$.
```

**"Do I need money to start?"**
```
No. It is free to play and free to verify.
```

**"How long does verify take?"**
```
About 30 seconds. One face scan. You only do it once.
```

**"Can I use two phones or two accounts?"**
```
One person, one player. The face check makes sure of that.
```

## Rules for posting in the group

- **One idea per message.** If it needs two paragraphs to explain, it is the
  wrong message for WhatsApp.
- **Never explain points in the group.** Anyone who wants the full rules can
  open the app, they are on the event page.
- **Lead with what they get, not how it works.** "Free 2,500 G$" gets read.
  "Every verified human you bring is worth 20 points" does not.
- **Answer questions in one line.** A long answer in a group chat reads as a
  new set of rules and confuses the next person.
