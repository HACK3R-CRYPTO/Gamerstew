## Q&A one-liners (1 min)

- **Why did wallets jump 72%?** "The consistency rewards story plus the perk shop launch — loyalty noticed publicly brings players back, and their friends with them."
- **What's the perk split to UBI?** "Today about a fifth of perk revenue routes to the GoodDollar UBI pool, the rest funds prize pools. We're formalizing the split with the GoodDollar team — that's part of my mentor ask."
- **How is it free / who pays the gas?** "We sponsor it. The backend signs and submits every score on Celo, so the player needs no CELO at all. Gas is fractions of a cent per game — it's the cheapest user acquisition we have, and it removed the biggest drop-off in onboarding."
- **How do you stop cheating?** "Server replays every run's input log before signing; the score can't reach the chain without that signature. And prizes only pay verified GoodDollar humans — a bot can play but can never get paid."
- **What's revenue?** "Perk sales in G$ — 617 purchases, 3,766 G$ in player spend already. Habitats and duels stack on top."
- **Solo, really?** "Solo on code. Preshie runs community. The players run the culture."
