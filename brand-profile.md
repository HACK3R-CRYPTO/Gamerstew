## 1. Brand — what it is
GameArena is a competitive gaming platform where verified humans play quick skill games
that make you sharper (memory, focus, patience) and challenge MARKOV, an autonomous
on-chain AI opponent that learns how you play. Live on Celo Mainnet at gamearenahq.xyz,
works in the browser and inside Opera MiniPay. Real people, no bots.

## 2. Audience
- Crypto Twitter · Celo, GoodDollar, MiniPay ecosystem builders and users
- Web3 gamers and AI-agent watchers
- Everyday mobile gamers in emerging markets (Africa / Nigeria especially — MiniPay's base)
All framed as verified humans, not bots.

## 3. Tone (three words)
warm · confident · playful
(Grounded hype — never corporate, never desperate. Confident silence over over-explaining.)

## 4. Platforms
Twitter/X (primary) · Telegram (community). Posting 1–2x/day, best Tue–Thu.

## 5. Standard CTAs (soft, never money-first)
- "Play at gamearenahq.xyz" / "come play" / "join the arena"
- "drop your @" (mutuals/engagement)
- "think you can out-read MARKOV?" (challenge)
Lead with the game and the people; money/rewards are a soft afterthought at most.

## 6. Banned words + rules (STRICT — the founder catches violations instantly)
- NO em dashes anywhere. Use middot ( · ), commas, or periods.
- NO money-first framing ("win USDC", "get rich", "earn big"). Social-first, skill-first only.
- NO invented features. MARKOV plays Rock-Paper-Scissors + Coin Flip ONLY (never Dice).
  Games are exactly Rhythm Rush, Simon Memory, Stack Tower. Challenge AI is free best-of-5.
- NO "the only project that…" or unverified superiority claims.
- NO buzzwords/cliches, no "synergy/ecosystem" filler, no setup language.
- Spartan, active voice, short impactful sentences, "you/your", bullet lists in posts.
- Emoji: sparing. 🎮 👑 🔥 🌞 fine; don't overuse.

## Core positioning (the angles to pull from)
1. Social-first, NOT money-first
2. Quick skill games that sharpen you (Simon=memory, Stack=patience, Rhythm=focus, MARKOV=reading your opponent)
3. "Reset, not escape" — a break that gives something back
4. Verified humans, no bots
5. MARKOV — an AI that learns how you think (the differentiator)
